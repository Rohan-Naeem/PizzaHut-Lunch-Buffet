Buffet Guide

Pizza Hut Buffet Hours

Typical lunch and dinner buffet times — and how to confirm your local restaurant's hours before you go.

The Pizza Hut buffet is a lunch buffet at most locations — there is no single, chain-wide buffet schedule. Each participating restaurant sets its own buffet days and hours, so the times below are typical ranges reported by customers, not official hours for every location. Always confirm with your local restaurant before visiting, especially if you are driving some distance for the buffet.

Typical Pizza Hut Lunch Buffet Hours

These are the windows customers most commonly report. Your local restaurant may run a shorter window, start later, or only offer the buffet on certain days.

Day Typical hours Notes
Monday – Friday 11:00 AM – 2:00 PM Most commonly reported lunch buffet window
Monday – Friday (some locations) 11:00 AM – 1:30 PM Shorter lunch window reported at some restaurants
Monday – Friday (some locations) 11:30 AM – 1:30 PM Later start, shorter window reported at some restaurants
Saturday – Sunday Varies by location Some restaurants run a weekend lunch buffet; others are weekdays only
Dinner buffet Not the norm A few locations have been reported to run a Tuesday dinner buffet — check locally

Is the Pizza Hut Buffet Lunch Only?

At most locations, yes. The buffet is built around the lunch crowd, which is why the typical window runs from late morning to early afternoon. A dinner buffet is the exception rather than the rule — a few locations have been reported to run one on Tuesdays, but that is not a chain-wide offer. If dinner is your plan, call the restaurant first rather than assuming the buffet will be running.

What Is Included During Buffet Hours?

During buffet hours, the line typically includes several pizza varieties, pasta, a salad bar, sides, wings and desserts — the full spread is on our Pizza Hut Buffet Menu page. The exact selection depends on the restaurant, and items are refreshed through the lunch window. For the full regular menu outside buffet hours, see the Full Pizza Hut Menu.

How to Find Your Local Pizza Hut Buffet Hours

Because hours vary by location, the only way to be sure is to check the restaurant itself:

  • Use the official Pizza Hut buffet page at pizzahut.com to find a participating restaurant near you.
  • Call the restaurant and ask whether the buffet is running today, and at what times.
  • Arrive early in the window — the fullest selection is usually at the start of the lunch service, and popular items can run out towards the end.
  • Check the price too: buffet pricing varies by location just like hours do. See our Pizza Hut Lunch Buffet guide for reference prices.
