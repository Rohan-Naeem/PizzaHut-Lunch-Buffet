Buffet Guide

Does Pizza Hut Have a Buffet?

Yes — at select locations. Here's where the buffet still runs, what's on it, and how to check your local restaurant.

Yes — Pizza Hut still has a buffet. The lunch buffet is back at select participating restaurants in the US, but it is not available at every Pizza Hut. Many locations — especially delivery and carryout-only restaurants — do not offer a buffet at all, and at most locations that do, it is lunch only. Before driving to a restaurant for the buffet, always check that your local restaurant is participating.

Where Is the Pizza Hut Buffet Available?

Pizza Hut keeps no public registry of buffet locations, so there is no official list to check. Buffets have been reported in these states, based on customer reports gathered in 2025 — treat this as a guide to where buffets are known to exist, not a guarantee that a particular restaurant near you has one:

Georgia, Indiana, Alabama, Kentucky, Virginia, Colorado, Kansas, Illinois, Michigan, Florida, Tennessee, Missouri, North Carolina, South Carolina, Montana, Ohio, Mississippi, Wyoming, Texas, Iowa, Pennsylvania, Hawaii, New Hampshire and California.

Even inside these states, only some restaurants run the buffet. A dine-in Pizza Hut in one of these states may still not offer it, and participation can change — restaurants add or drop the buffet over time. The only way to know for sure is to ask your local restaurant.

What Is on the Pizza Hut Buffet?

During buffet hours, the line typically includes several pizza varieties, pasta, a salad bar, sides, wings and desserts — the full spread is on our Pizza Hut Buffet Menu page. The exact selection depends on the restaurant, and items are refreshed through the lunch window. Outside buffet hours, the same restaurant serves the regular menu — see the Full Pizza Hut Menu.

How Much Does the Pizza Hut Buffet Cost?

There is no single buffet price. Each restaurant sets its own price, and it can change without notice. Customers have reported a typical range of about $9.99 to $14.99 per person — that is a reported range only, not an official price, and your local restaurant may charge more or less. Some locations also charge a different price for children. Confirm the price with your local restaurant before visiting — see our Pizza Hut Lunch Buffet guide for reference prices.

When Is the Pizza Hut Buffet Served?

At most locations the buffet is a lunch service. The most commonly reported window is 11:00 AM to 2:00 PM on weekdays, though some restaurants run shorter windows and some only offer the buffet on select days. A dinner buffet is the exception rather than the rule. Full details are on our Pizza Hut Buffet Hours page.

How to Check Your Local Pizza Hut Buffet

Because participation, hours and prices are all set by each restaurant, the only way to be sure is to check the restaurant itself:

  • Use the official Pizza Hut buffet page at pizzahut.com to find a participating restaurant near you.
  • Call the restaurant and ask whether the buffet is running today, at what times, and what it costs.
  • Ask about the days too — some restaurants run the buffet on weekdays only, others include weekends.
  • Arrive early in the window — the fullest selection is usually at the start of the lunch service.
