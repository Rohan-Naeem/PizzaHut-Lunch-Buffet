Buffet Basics

What is the Pizza Hut Lunch Buffet?

An all-you-care-to-eat lineup of pizza, sides, pasta, wings, and dessert offered at participating restaurants during set hours.

Dine-In Experience

The buffet is generally a dine-in feature. Availability is restaurant-specific, so it may not be offered (or may be offered differently) at every location.

Buffet Hours

Buffet days and hours vary by restaurant — some locations run it daily at lunch, others on select days only. There is no single universal buffet time.

Pricing & Selection

Price, food selection, and participation can vary by location and can change without notice. Always confirm with your local restaurant.
