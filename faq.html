Pizza Hut Lunch Buffet — FAQ

Answers to common questions about the buffet.

Buffet availability depends on the participating location — some restaurants offer it, others don't. Check with your local Pizza Hut to confirm whether the buffet is currently offered.

A typical buffet includes several pizza varieties along with sides, pasta, wings, salad, and dessert, though the exact selection depends on the restaurant.

Pricing varies by location and can change over time. Treat any price shown here as a reference/typical price, and confirm the current price with your local restaurant.

Buffet days and hours vary by restaurant — there is no single universal schedule. Contact your local Pizza Hut for its specific buffet hours.

Drink inclusion can vary by location and current offer. Some locations may include a drink, others may charge separately — check locally.
