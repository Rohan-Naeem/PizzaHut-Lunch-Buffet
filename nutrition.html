Pizza Hut Buffet Calories & Nutrition

Understand how calories can add up on the buffet line.

How Buffet Calories Work

  • Calories vary by food. A slice of veggie pizza and a slice of meat lover's pizza can differ by 100+ calories.
  • Portion size matters. Plate size and how many slices, wings, or scoops you take directly affects total intake.
  • Toppings add up. Extra cheese, meats, or sauces increase calories, fat, and sodium.
  • Sauces and dressings count too. Ranch, BBQ, and Caesar dressing can add significant calories.
  • Nutrition can vary by product and preparation, and menu items and recipes can change over time.
Calorie values shown across this site are approximate, reference estimates based on typical serving sizes. They are not official nutrition facts and can vary by location, preparation, and recipe changes.
